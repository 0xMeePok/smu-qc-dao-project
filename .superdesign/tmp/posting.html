Problem statement

Route optimisation for cold-chain delivery under demand spikes

Submitted
OverviewProposalsMatch

The problem

Current cold-chain logistics rely on static routing models that fail to account for dynamic demand spikes in urban centers. This leads to increased fuel consumption, equipment wear, and a significant risk of product spoilage due to thermal deviations during extended transit times.

We need a predictive model that integrates real-time demand data with multi-objective route optimization algorithms to minimize both cost and carbon footprint while guaranteeing 99.9% thermal stability.

Current approach

We are currently using a modified VRP (Vehicle Routing Problem) heuristic developed in 2018. It lacks awareness of real-time traffic congestion and doesn't dynamically reroute based on warehouse inventory fluctuations. This rigidity results in a 12% inefficiency gap compared to theoretical optimal performance during peak seasons.

What success looks like

Success is defined by the delivery of a production-ready API that provides route re-calculation in under 5 seconds. The model must demonstrate a minimum 15% reduction in total distance traveled across a 30-day pilot while maintaining zero thermal incidents. Documentation must include a full audit trail of the decision-making logic for regulatory compliance.
